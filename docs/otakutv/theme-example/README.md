# OtakuTV example theme

Copy this folder **outside** the repository, add your own pictures to `wallpapers/` with
`tools/wallpaper_prep.py`, name them in `wallpapers/captions.txt`, then push it with `tools/theme.ps1`.
Step by step: [INSTALL.md, section 7](../INSTALL.md#7-your-anime-wallpapers).

What `config.json` turns on: a new picture every 6 seconds with a slide, bilingual captions, the weather
under the date (`header_weather`, needs the Weather add-on), a frosted-glass dock, the glass focus highlight
with its spring, and the app library. No pictures ship here: bring your own.
